Privacy · retention · boundaries

Minimal retention
by default.

What Elenchi keeps, for how long, and how to delete it.

01

Custom prompts are not stored by default

A custom prompt is reduced to an irreversible policyHash and discarded after the run — unless you enable 24-hour AES-256-GCM encrypted retention for server-side patching. Never paste private keys, seed phrases or credentials.

02

Private by default, deletable instantly

Runs default to private and are bound to an HttpOnly, HMAC-signed session cookie. “Delete this run” removes the record and any encrypted prompt immediately.

03

Model provider disclosure

Live runs send the active policy prompt and the synthetic scenario to the configured provider (Cloudflare Workers AI or OpenRouter/OpenAI) to generate challenger and defender turns. Provider keys stay server-side.

04

Independence and scope

Elenchi is not affiliated with or endorsed by Robinhood, Pons, Blockscout or Sourcify. A passing result never guarantees immunity against novel attacks, model drift, or missing wallet-level guardrails.
